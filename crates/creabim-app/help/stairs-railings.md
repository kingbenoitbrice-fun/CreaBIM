# Stairs and railings

## Stairs

1. Pick the **Stair** tool.
2. Click where the stair starts, then click in the direction it climbs.

It rises to the next level in equal risers no higher than *Max riser*. *Properties* shows the number of risers, their height and the 2R + G comfort rule. Tick *Fixed rise* to type a height instead.

Turn on **L-shaped** for a landing: set after how many risers it comes and which way the stair turns.

In plan, a stair is drawn with its risers below the cut, a break line, the treads above the cut dashed, and a walking line with an arrow and *UP*. On the level it climbs to, the whole stair is drawn where its well lets you see it, with *DN*; the plan's *View depth* sets how far down it looks (see [Views](views#plan-graphics)).

> **Note:** A stair doesn't cut the slab above by itself. Draw a [shaft](holes#shafts) for its stair well.

## Railings

- Pick the **Railing** tool and click inside a stair: it gets a railing on both sides, following the flights and the landing. Or use *Add railings* in the stair's *Properties*.
- Or click the points of a path and click the last point again to finish.

Railings on a stair belong to it: they move with it and are deleted with it.
