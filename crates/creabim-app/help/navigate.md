# Moving around the views

## In the 3D view

| Do this | To |
| --- | --- |
| Drag with the left button | Orbit round the model |
| Drag with the right button | Pan |
| Roll the wheel | Zoom |
| `Home` | Frame everything |
| `.` | Frame the selection |

The buttons at the top right of the 3D view do the same, and more: zoom in and out, orbit round the selection, perspective or orthographic, the ground grid, [X-ray](visibility#x-ray), **Show** (what the 3D view shows) and **View** (look from the top, the front, a side or a corner).

## In plans, sections and sheets

| Do this | To |
| --- | --- |
| Drag with the right button | Pan |
| Roll the wheel | Zoom |

## Layout

- *View › One view at a time*, or the square button by the view switcher, gives one big view with tabs: *3D view* and the open drawing. *Split* goes back to both.
- *View › Docked layout* puts the views side by side or one above the other, and you drag the divider between them to share the room. Double-click it to reset.
- *View › In its own window* moves the 2D view to a window of its own, for a second screen.
- *View › Overlaid in the 3D workspace* shows the 2D view as an inset over the 3D view.
- *View › Show the 2D view in 3D space* draws the plan or section in the 3D view, where it cuts.

## The ground grid

The 3D view shows a grid on the working level. Turn it off with the grid button in its navigation bar, and pick its spacing in *View › Ground grid*, including any spacing you type.
