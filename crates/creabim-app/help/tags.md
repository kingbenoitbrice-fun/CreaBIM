# Tags

A **tag** is a label that reads from the element it points at: a door's mark (D01), a window's (W01), a wall's material and thickness, a roof's thickness and pitch, a column's profile, a beam's size. Change the element, and its tag changes.

## Tagging

1. Pick the **Tag** tool.
2. Click a door, window, wall, slab, roof, column, beam, stair or railing.
3. Click where its tag goes.

**Tag openings** in the top bar tags every door and window in the view that has no tag yet.

## Moving and styling

- Drag a tag to move it. It moves with its element too.
- Pick a **Style** in the tool options for new tags, or change a tag's style in *Properties*. A style sets the tag's shape (circle, hexagon and others), text and colour. See [Annotation styles](styles).

Deleting an element deletes its tags.
