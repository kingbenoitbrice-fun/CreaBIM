# Step 6: look at it

Everything you see in CreaBIM is drawn from the model. You never draw a section by hand: you say where to cut, and CreaBIM draws it.

## Turn around in 3D

In the 3D view, drag with the left mouse button to orbit, drag with the right button to pan, and roll the wheel to zoom. `Home` frames the whole model. See [Moving around the views](navigate).

## Cut a section

1. Open the floor plan.
2. Pick the **Section** tool.
3. Click two points across the house. The section looks to the **left** of the direction you draw.
4. Open the new section from the project browser (under *Sections*), or from the view switcher at the top right.

Cut walls and slabs are drawn heavy and hatched; what lies beyond is drawn in thinner lines. Elevations work the same way with the **Elevation** tool: draw them outside the building.

![A section, made from the model](img/section.jpg)

Change a wall, and the plan, the section and the 3D view all change with it.
