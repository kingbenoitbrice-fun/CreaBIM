# Furniture and fixtures

Furniture, kitchen and bathroom fixtures and other objects that are placed in a building rather than built.

## Until then

- Bring objects in from Blender or FreeCAD with *File › Import and link…*. Give them an IFC class such as `IfcFurniture` and they are exported as that. See [Blender](blender) and [FreeCAD](freecad).
- Opening an IFC file brings its furniture in as meshes that keep their IFC class. See [IFC](ifc).
- Or model a simple object as a [mass](masses).
