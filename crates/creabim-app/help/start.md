# Getting started: a first house

This short tour builds a small one-storey house from scratch, and ends with a printed sheet and an IFC file. Each step takes a few minutes. You can stop at any point and come back.

1. [Start a project](start-project): a new empty project, or a sample to look at.
2. [Set up the levels](start-levels): the floors of the building.
3. [Draw the walls](start-walls): the outline of the house.
4. [Add doors and windows](start-openings): they cut their own holes.
5. [Add a floor and a roof](start-floors-roofs): slabs and roofs from an outline.
6. [Look at it: plans, sections and 3D](start-views): views made from the model.
7. [Put it on a sheet](start-sheets): a drawing ready to print.
8. [Save and export](start-export): the project file, PDF and IFC.

> **Tip:** Want to see a finished model first? CreaBIM opens on [the starter house](starter-house), and *File › New starter house* brings it back. Everything in this tour is already in it, so you can click around and see how it is made.
