# Step 5: add a floor and a roof

## The floor slab

A **slab** is a floor (or a ceiling, or a terrace): a flat plate drawn from its outline.

1. Pick the **Slab** tool.
2. In the bar under the tools, pick **Fill room** (or press `3`).
3. Click inside your walls. A slab fills the outline of the room.

Other ways to draw it: **Polygon** (`1`, click each corner), **Rectangle** (`2`, two corners) and **From sketch** (`4`). See [Slabs](slabs).

## The roof

1. Add a level for the top of the walls if you haven't (see [Levels](levels)), or set the roof's *Base offset* afterwards.
2. Pick the **Roof** tool.
3. In the bar, choose its shape: **Flat**, **Mono-pitch**, **Gable** or **Hip**, and its **Pitch** (the slope in degrees).
4. Draw its outline like a slab: **Rectangle** round the house is the quickest.

![Roofs on the sample house](img/roofs.jpg)

The roof's overhang, thickness and which edges are eaves are in its *Properties*. See [Roofs](roofs).

> **Tip:** Want the walls to stop neatly under a sloped roof? Select the walls and use *Attach top to roof/slab* in *Properties* or the right-click menu.
