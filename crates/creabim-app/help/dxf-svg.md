# PDF, SVG and DXF

## PDF

*File › Sheets as PDF* writes every [sheet](sheets) as a page of one vector PDF, with hatches, dimensions, notes in their real fonts and shaded views. Ready to print or send.

## SVG

*File › This view or sheet as SVG* writes the drawing open in the 2D pane:

- a sheet, at its paper size;
- a plan, section or elevation, at its scale.

SVG opens in Inkscape, Illustrator, Affinity and web browsers, for presentation work.

## DXF and SVG title blocks

*File › Sheet templates… › Import DXF or SVG…* brings in your own title block or sheet frame from a CAD drawing. Any `{name}` in its text becomes a field. See [Sheets and title blocks](sheets#title-blocks-and-templates).

> **Note:** CreaBIM doesn't write DXF yet. To get drawings into a DXF-only program, export SVG or PDF, or the model as [IFC](ifc).
