# Edit mode

Edit mode opens one element so every one of its grips shows, in every view it is in: plans of each level it passes through, sections, elevations and 3D. Use it to reshape what was drawn, not just move it.

## Opening and closing it

- Select the element, then click **Edit** at the top of *Properties*;
- or double-click the selected element in any view;
- or press `Enter` with it selected.

`Esc`, `Enter` again, *Finish editing*, or selecting something else closes it. *Properties* says *Editing* while it is open, and the status bar lists what you can do.

## Outlines

Slabs, roofs, shafts, the holes in slabs and roofs, and sketches show their outlines traced in orange, with:

- a **square grip** on each corner: drag it to move the corner;
- a **diamond** (◇) in the middle of each edge: drag it to pull a new corner out of the edge, or click it to add one there;
- a **round grip** just outside each edge: drag it to push the edge out or pull it in, square to itself;
- **double-click a corner** to take it away. An outline keeps at least three corners.

A sketch with arcs or constraints keeps its corners: you can drag them, but not add or remove them.

## Shafts

A shaft's corners and the two round grips in its middle (its **base** and its **top**) show whenever it is selected; Edit mode adds its edges and add-corner diamonds. Every slab and roof it cuts follows as you drag. See [Shafts](holes#shafts).

## Other elements

Walls, doors, windows, wall openings, columns, beams, stairs and the rest show their usual [handles](handles) in Edit mode, in every view.
