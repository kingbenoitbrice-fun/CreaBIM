# About CreaBIM

CreaBIM {version} is an open-source, BIM-native architectural modeller: one model for freeform concept design and permit drawings, with Blender-style modelling, live 2D views and native IFC.

It is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. You may use it for any purpose, study and change its source code, and share it, with or without your changes, under the same licence.

Its interface icons come from Lucide (ISC licence).

## This guide

The pages of this guide are plain Markdown files in the `crates/creabim-app/help` folder of the source code, built into the app. Found something unclear or out of date? Changing a page is as easy as editing a text file.
