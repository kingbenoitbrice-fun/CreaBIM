# Step 4: add doors and windows

Doors and windows live *in* a wall. They cut their own opening, and they move with the wall.

1. Pick the **Door** tool.
2. Move the pointer over a wall in the plan or in 3D. A preview shows where the door goes.
3. Click to place it.
4. Pick the **Window** tool and click on walls to place windows the same way.

## Adjust them

- With the **Select** tool, click a door or window. Set its *Width*, *Height* and *Sill* (how high a window starts above the floor) in *Properties*.
- Drag it along its wall to move it.
- Press `Space` to flip which way a door opens. `Shift+Space` flips its hinge side. Or click the orange flip arrows beside it in the plan.
