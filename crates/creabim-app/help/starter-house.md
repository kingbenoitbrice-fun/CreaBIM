# The starter house

CreaBIM opens on the **Maple Lane House**, a two-storey family house with a single-storey kitchen wing. It is a complete small project: the model, the drawings made from it and a set of sheets ready to print. Nothing in it is special. It is built with the same tools you have, so you can take it apart to see how it works, or change it and watch the drawings follow. To get it back, use *File › New starter house*.

## What is in the model

- **Levels**: *Foundation* (-800), *Ground Floor* (±0) and *First Floor* (+3000). See [Levels](levels).
- **Grids** A–E and 1–3, which the dimensions measure from. See [Grids](grids).
- **Walls** built from [layered assemblies](assemblies): white render or timber cladding on insulation and block, with stud partitions upstairs. The walls join at corners and tees. The first-floor walls follow the roof up into the gables. See [Walls](walls).
- **Doors and windows** that share [types](types), with frames. Some are flipped to open the other way. A plain opening joins the kitchen to the living room. See [Doors and windows](doors-windows).
- **A curtain wall**: the three-bay garden glazing in the kitchen wing, with a door in its middle bay. It cuts its own opening in the north wall. See [Curtain walls](curtain-walls).
- **Floors**: an insulated ground slab, a concrete first floor with a hole for the service riser, and paving for the terrace, the loggia steps and the front path. See [Slabs](slabs).
- **A stair** turning at a landing, with a handrail and a balustrade. A **shaft** cuts the stair well through the first floor. See [Stairs and railings](stairs-railings).
- **Roofs**: a slate gable roof at 35°, a green flat roof over the wing and the terrace, and a zinc canopy over the front door. See [Roofs](roofs).
- **Structure**: a steel column at the loggia corner that rises through both floors, two terrace posts, and steel beams under the balcony and along the eaves. See [Columns and beams](columns-beams).
- **Footings**: concrete strips under the walls and pads under the columns. See [Footings](footings).
- **Furniture and fixtures** from the catalogue in every room: kitchen units, sofas, beds, a bath and WCs, sockets, switches, lights, radiators and a boiler. See [Furniture and fixtures](furniture) and [Electrical, plumbing and HVAC fixtures](fixtures).

## The drawings

The project browser lists the views. Each one is made from the model, so it updates as soon as you change something.

- **Plans** of the ground floor, the first floor, the foundation and the roof. They show room names with their floor areas, tags on every door and window, and chains of dimensions. See [Dimensions](dimensions) and [Tags](tags).
- **Sections** A-A through the stair and B-B through the loggia and the wing. See [Sections and elevations](sections).
- **Elevations** of all four sides, shaded.
- **3D views** from the street and from the garden.
- **Schedules** of doors, windows, walls, structure, furniture and fixtures. See [Schedules](schedules).

## The sheets

The *Sheets* group holds a set of drawings like one sent for planning permission. Each sheet has the project's own title block, filled in with the job, the client and the status. See [Sheets](sheets).

- A000 Cover, with the garden view and the drawing list
- A101 Ground Floor Plan and A102 First Floor Plan, 1:50
- A103 Foundation Plan, 1:50, and A104 Roof Plan, 1:100
- A201 Section A-A and A202 Section B-B, 1:50
- A301 Elevations, 1:100
- A601 and A602 Schedules
- A901 3D Views

## Things to try

- Select a wall in the 3D view and drag its end. The floors, the dimensions and the room around it follow in every view.
- Pick a window and pick another type in the **Type** picker in *Properties*. Its tag and the window schedule change too.
- Print the whole set with *File › Sheets as PDF*. See [Save and export](start-export).
- Right-click a roof in the 3D view and pick *Hide category in view* to look inside. See [Visibility and X-ray](visibility).
- Export the house with *File › IFC4 (.ifc)* and open it in another program. See [IFC](ifc).

> **Tip:** The room names and areas are notes written in the plans. If you move a wall, update the area in the note by hand.
