# Step 3: draw the walls

1. Open the floor plan of *Level 0*.
2. Pick the **Wall** tool in the tool row (the brick icon), or *Create › Wall*.
3. Look at the bar under the tools. It shows the wall's **Thickness**, its **Location line** and which side is outside. Type a thickness such as `300`.
4. Click in the plan where the first corner goes. Move the pointer and click again: that is one wall. Keep clicking to draw the next walls of the chain.
5. To close the outline, click on the first corner again. Press `Esc` or right-click to stop.

![The Wall tool and its options](img/wall-options.jpg)

## Drawing exactly

- Hold `Shift` to keep the wall straight along an axis.
- To set exact ends afterwards, select the wall and type its *Start* and *End* in *Properties*.
- Lean on guide lines: hold the pointer still on a corner for a moment (or press `Q`) and dashed lines run from it, level, plumb and at 45°. See [Guides](guides).
- The pointer **snaps** to corners, midpoints and other walls. A small marker and label (such as *Endpoint*) show what it caught. See [Snaps](snaps).
- Walls that meet join cleanly by themselves: corners are mitred and a wall that ends on another makes a clean T.

Made a mistake? `Ctrl+Z` takes it back. To change a wall later, pick the **Select** tool (the arrow), click the wall and edit it in the *Properties* panel on the right, or drag its square grips.
