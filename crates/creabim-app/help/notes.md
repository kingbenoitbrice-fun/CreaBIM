# Notes

A **note** is free text on a drawing, with or without a leader arrow pointing at something.

## Writing a note

1. Pick the **Note** tool.
2. With **Leader** on (in the tool options), click what the note points at, then where its text goes. With *Leader* off, one click places the text.
3. Type the text in *Properties*: the text box is ready to type into. `Enter` adds a line.

If the leader's point snapped onto an element, it follows that element when it moves.

## On sheets

Pick the Note tool on a sheet, outside any viewport, to write on the paper itself: general notes, title notes.

## Moving and styling

With the Select tool, drag a note's text to move it, its square grip at the arrow to move the leader's end, and its round grip to bend the leader at an elbow (**Straighten the leader** in *Properties* takes the elbow out). **Leader end** in *Properties* picks an arrow, a dot or nothing for this note. Pick a **Style**, or an *Own colour*, as for dimensions. See [Annotation styles](styles).

Notes are a visibility category: a view can hide them (see [Visibility](visibility)).
