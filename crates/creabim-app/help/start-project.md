# Step 1: start a project

## A new empty project

Pick *File › New empty project*. You get one level, *Level 0*, with its floor plan, and nothing else. The status bar says what to do next.

## Or open a sample

The *File* menu has a few ready-made houses to explore:

- *New sample house*: a two-storey house with walls, doors, windows, slabs, a roof, a stair, sections, elevations, schedules and sheets.
- *New layered sample house*: the same house built from layered walls, floors and roof (see [Layered assemblies](assemblies)).
- *New typed sample house*: walls, doors and windows that share [types](types), a storefront [curtain wall](curtain-walls), a wall opening and a stair shaft.
- *New sample with a beam in a wall*: a lintel hidden inside a wall, to try [X-ray](visibility#x-ray) and [picking what is hidden](select#picking-what-is-underneath).

## Choose your units

Before drawing, check *File › Project units…*. Pick **Metric** (millimetres, centimetres or metres) or **Imperial** (feet and inches). Every field, dimension and schedule follows that choice. See [Units](units).

## The two views

The middle of the window shows the **3D view** and a **2D view** (a plan, section or sheet) side by side. Whatever you draw in one appears in the other at once. The button left of the view switcher (top right) shows one view at a time instead, with tabs to switch.
